Stream quality
How much your server is asked to send, on every channel. High is 1080p at 12 Mbps, Medium 720p at 4 Mbps, Low 480p at 1.5 Mbps. Applies from the next program.
Sleep timer
Stops playback when time's up, after a 20 second "Still watching?" check. Keeps counting when you change channels.
Playback information
How this program is playing: direct or through your server, player size, source codecs, and why a fallback happened.
