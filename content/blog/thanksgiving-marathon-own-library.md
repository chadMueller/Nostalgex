# A Thanksgiving TV marathon from your own library

> Nostalgex has a TURKEY DAY channel that finds the Thanksgiving episodes in your own Plex, Jellyfin or Emby library and runs them back to back, like a holiday marathon on cable. It's part of the TIS THE SEASON bundle, which Nostalgex offers from a week before US Thanksgiving. You can also turn the bundle on yourself in Settings, and it fills itself from the shows you already own. It's free on Apple TV, Roku and in the web tuner.

Every family has a Thanksgiving TV tradition. In my house it was the parade for about eleven minutes, then football for the uncles, then whoever got the remote first flipping until they landed on a sitcom's Thanksgiving episode. Somebody's turkey was always on fire. Somebody was always stuck in a car on the way to dinner. It was comfort food for your eyes.

If you've got a library full of old sitcoms, you already own most of that marathon. You just can't find it, because nobody remembers which season of which show has the Thanksgiving one.

## How do I watch every Thanksgiving episode I own in a row?

Let Nostalgex find them. TURKEY DAY is a channel that looks through your TV shows for episodes about Thanksgiving: an episode with Thanksgiving, Friendsgiving or Turkey Day in its title, a November episode with turkeys or pilgrims in the title, or an episode whose summary mentions Thanksgiving. Then it puts them on a schedule and keeps them running, so you tune in mid episode like real TV.

You don't make a playlist. You don't hunt through seasons. If you own the episodes, they show up.

## What kind of episodes end up on TURKEY DAY?

Whatever Thanksgiving episodes your library has. If you're a sitcom household, that might mean things like:

* **Friends**, The One with All the Thanksgivings
* **Cheers**, Thanksgiving Orphans
* **How I Met Your Mother**, Slapsgiving
* **Gilmore Girls**, A Deep-Fried Korean Thanksgiving
* **Bob's Burgers**, An Indecent Thanksgiving Proposal

That's just a taste, and only if you own them. TURKEY DAY needs at least five Thanksgiving episodes before it airs, so a small library might not get one. It sticks to TV episodes, so the Thanksgiving movies stay on their regular channels. Planes, Trains and Automobiles might still turn up on NOSTALGEX LOL or 80S LOL while you wait for the pie.

## What does a whole Thanksgiving day look like?

Here's how I'd flip through the day. You don't schedule any of this. The channels are already running, so you just tune to the one that fits the hour.

* **Morning: the parade, then cartoons.** Watch the real parade live like always. When the last balloon goes by, flip to SAT MORNING CARTOONS or the KIDZ ZONE channels so the kids are busy while the oven does its thing.
* **Noon: football.** The actual game is on regular TV. For the rest of the day, LOCKERROOM in the OVERTIME bundle runs the sports movies in your library, so if you own a few football movies, that's your halftime show.
* **After dinner: sitcoms.** This is TURKEY DAY's moment. Every Thanksgiving episode you own, back to back, while everyone argues about pie.
* **Late night: whatever you land on.** 90S SITCOMS, NOSTALGEX LOL, VHS VAULT. Flip until somebody falls asleep on the couch. Somebody always does.

## A few tips for the big day

* **Kids at the table?** On Apple TV and in the web tuner, the WATERSHED switch in Settings holds R, 18 and TV-MA titles until 9 PM on every channel. It's off unless you turn it on.
* **Falling asleep after dinner is tradition.** The sleep timer in the Now Playing panel on Apple TV and in the web tuner turns things off after 15, 30 or 60 minutes.
* **Canadian?** You had your Thanksgiving in October, I know. TURKEY DAY runs on the US calendar, but nobody's stopping you from a second helping.

## FAQ

### How do I make a Thanksgiving episode marathon from my Plex library?

Turn on the TIS THE SEASON bundle in Nostalgex. Its TURKEY DAY channel finds the Thanksgiving episodes in your Plex, Jellyfin or Emby library and runs them on a schedule.

### When does the TURKEY DAY channel show up?

Nostalgex offers the TIS THE SEASON bundle from a week before US Thanksgiving. You can switch it on yourself in Settings in any month.

### Why don't I have a TURKEY DAY channel?

It needs at least five Thanksgiving episodes in your library, found by episode title, November air date or summary. Fewer than that and the channel waits.

### Does TURKEY DAY play Thanksgiving movies?

No. It's for TV episodes. Movies stay on the regular channels.

### Does Nostalgex come with these episodes?

No. It only plays what's on your own Plex, Jellyfin or Emby server.
