# 80s sitcoms that make a great channel

> The 80s sitcoms that make the best channel are the comfortable, rewatchable ones: Cheers, The Golden Girls, Family Ties, Growing Pains, Who's the Boss?, ALF, Night Court, Perfect Strangers and Full House. If you own them on Plex, Jellyfin or Emby, Nostalgex puts their 80s episodes on the 80S SITCOMS channel (CH 20) by itself and keeps it running all day. Free on Apple TV, Roku and in the web tuner.

There's a specific kind of TV that only existed in the 80s. A living room set with a staircase nobody uses. A studio audience that laughs a half second too long. A theme song that tells you the whole premise. And a very special episode every season that made you sit very still on the carpet.

That's not a show you pick. That's a show you land on. Which is why 80s sitcoms make such a good channel.

## What makes a sitcom good for a 24/7 channel?

Three things, in my experience:

1. **You can join mid episode.** No season long arcs to follow. Somebody's in trouble, somebody learns a lesson, credits.
2. **A big episode count.** A channel needs a lot to draw from or you'll see the same three episodes all week.
3. **Comfort over cliffhangers.** You want it on while you fold laundry, not something you have to pause.

## The 80s sitcoms I'd want on the channel

These are my picks, not a ranking of anything. Just the ones that feel like 7:30 on a weeknight.

* **Cheers.** The bar where everybody knows your name. Endlessly rewatchable.
* **The Golden Girls.** Cheesecake at the kitchen table. Some of the sharpest writing of the decade.
* **Family Ties.** Alex P. Keaton and his briefcase.
* **Growing Pains.** The Seavers, the living room, the hair.
* **Who's the Boss?** Tony, Angela and a very fair question.
* **ALF.** A puppet from Melmac eating cats (allegedly). Peak 80s.
* **Night Court.** The weirdest courtroom in Manhattan.
* **Perfect Strangers.** The dance of joy, alone, is worth it.
* **Full House.** The late 80s seasons, before it rolled into the 90s.
* **Newhart.** The inn, the brothers, the ending everybody talks about.
