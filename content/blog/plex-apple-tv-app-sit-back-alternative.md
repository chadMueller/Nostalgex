# Missing something in the new Plex app on Apple TV? A sit back alternative

> Plex's redesigned app started rolling out on Apple TV on September 17, 2026. Watch Together isn't part of the new experience, the trailer button is gone (trailers now sit further down a movie's page), and some people report missing library filters like actor, director and year. If you mostly want to browse, those are worth raising with Plex. If you'd rather not browse at all, Nostalgex reads the same Plex server and turns it into live channels with a retro guide, so something's already on.

First, a confession: I like Plex. I've run a Plex server for years, and I'm still running one. So this isn't a "Plex is ruined" post. There are plenty of those on Reddit this month and I don't think we need another.

But a lot of people in those threads are saying the same thing in different ways: "I just want to turn on the TV and watch something." That part I can help with, because it's the whole reason I made Nostalgex. It's plug and play: open the app, scan a code with your phone, and the TV is already on.

## What changed in the new Plex app on Apple TV?

Here's what I could verify, with where it comes from:

* **The rollout.** Plex announced on [its Apple TV forum thread](https://forums.plex.tv/t/plex-for-apple-tv/121357/175) on September 17, 2026 that the "newly reimagined and redesigned Plex experience is starting to roll out on tvOS."
* **Watch Together.** Plex said back in February 2025 that Watch Together wouldn't be part of the new Plex experience on most devices, though it stays in the web app ([Android Authority's write up](https://www.androidauthority.com/plex-watch-together-removed-3529939/)). Plex also said it might offer something similar again in the future. Apple TV users are now [asking for it on the Plex forums](https://forums.plex.tv/t/missing-watch-together-feature-in-updated-appletv-app/943263).
* **Trailers.** They're not gone, but the trailer button is. People on r/PleX [describe scrolling to a trailer row near the bottom of the page](https://www.reddit.com/r/PleX/comments/1wtg9yx/apple_tv_app_trailers/), or going through More.
* **Library filters.** Some users [report losing filters by actor, director and year](https://www.reddit.com/r/PleX/comments/1wqjj6w/apple_tv_app_and_lost_functionality/). That's from user reports, and Plex has been shipping updates since the rollout, so check your own app before you take my word for it.

To be fair to Plex, they've been listening on some of it. Their August [navigation update](https://forums.plex.tv/t/navigation-updates-coming-to-preview-users/941751) moved the main menu back to the left side after "consistent feedback from the start regarding the navigation."

## FAQ

### Did Plex remove trailers from the Apple TV app?

Not entirely. The trailer button is gone in the new app, and users say trailers now sit in a row lower on the page or under More.

### Is Watch Together in the new Plex app on Apple TV?

No. Plex said Watch Together wouldn't be part of the new Plex experience on most devices. It's still in Plex's web app.

### Can I still filter my Plex library by actor or director on Apple TV?

Some users report those filters are missing in the redesigned app. Plex keeps shipping updates, so check the current version on your Apple TV.

### Does Nostalgex replace the Plex app?

Not really. It's a different way to watch the same server. Use Plex when you want to pick something, and Nostalgex when you want something to already be on.

### Does Nostalgex need a Plex Pass?

Not on your home network.
