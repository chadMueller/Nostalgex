Plug and play on Android TV.

Nostalgex turns your own Plex, Jellyfin or Emby library into live TV on Android TV.

Not on Google Play yet. Watch on Apple TV, Roku or in the web tuner today.

Real TV, from your own shelf.

  • LOCAL WEATHER, channel 299: a weather channel that loops current conditions, today's forecast, a 5-day forecast and radar, with its own music. Set your city or postcode in Settings, Channels & Bundles, Local Weather, and pick Fahrenheit or Celsius or leave it on Auto. It sits in the guide as one long bar. Radar covers the United States, Canada and Germany.
  • Your movies and shows get sorted into 120+ themed channels, like the back of an old cable bill.
  • Every channel plays on a set daily schedule, so something is already on when you tune in.
  • The guide shows what's on every channel right now and what's coming up.
  • Select on the bundle strip in the guide jumps to the first channel of that bundle.
  • CHOOSE YOUR LIBRARIES: turn off any library, hidden Jellyfin ones too, before the first scan or in Settings.
  • Progress shows inside Settings when you switch servers.
  • Sign in to Plex with the code on screen, or scan the QR code with your phone. Jellyfin and Emby look for your server on your network, or you can type its address.
  • Nostalgex is free and open source.

Android TV questions, answered.

Is it on Google Play?

Not yet. There is no download link for Android TV today. Apple TV, Roku and the web tuner are available now.

Does it cost anything?

Nostalgex is free.

Do I need to set up a server or Docker?

You need your own Plex, Jellyfin or Emby server with media on it. Nostalgex itself needs nothing installed.
