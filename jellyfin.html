You self-host for a reason. Nothing leaves your house.

Typing a server password into somebody else's app is a fair thing to be suspicious about. So here is exactly what Nostalgex does with it.

  • Your server address and token are stored in the Apple TV Keychain and are never sent to us
  • Sign-in goes straight from the app to your server. We never see your username or password
  • We do not host, stream, or supply any media. Every frame comes off your own machine
  • No account to create, no ads, nothing sold to anyone
  • The app sends a short fixed list of anonymous usage events, like a channel being tuned. Never titles, library contents, or your server address

Quick Connect

Approve the Apple TV from a browser you are already signed into on your Jellyfin server. No typing a long password with the remote.

Your collections become channels

A collection on your server turns into its own channel, sitting alongside the hundred-plus that Nostalgex builds from genres, eras and franchises.

Subtitles and audio tracks

Pick a subtitle and audio language once and the app remembers it. Files with several audio tracks behave the way you would expect.

Over a hundred channels

Sorted into 14 bundles you can switch on and off. Turn the whole kids bundle off, or keep only the decades.

Plays your file as-is

Direct play when the Apple TV can decode it, and it only falls back to transcoding when it has to. Movies split across two files play straight through as one programme.

No Apple TV? There's a free web tuner.

The same channels, running in any browser. It is the fastest way to see what this is before installing anything.

One caveat worth knowing up front. Browsers block a secure page from talking to a plain http server, so the web tuner needs your Jellyfin server on an https address. If yours is http on the local network, use the Apple TV app instead. It has no such restriction.

Quick answers.

Does Nostalgex work with Jellyfin?

Yes, natively. The Apple TV app talks to Jellyfin directly, the same way it talks to Plex and Emby. There is no bridge, no middleman service, and no Plex account involved.

Do I have to type my password with the remote?

No. Jellyfin's Quick Connect is supported, so you approve the Apple TV from a browser that is already signed in. Typing a server address and password still works if you prefer it.

Does it change anything on my server?

It reads your library to build the channels. It never adds, renames, moves or deletes anything.

Do I pick what plays on each channel?

No, and that's on purpose. Nostalgex reads your library and builds the channels and schedules itself. The surprise of "oh, this is on" is the product.

What do I need?

An Apple TV running tvOS 18 or later, and a Jellyfin server you can reach from it. That's the whole list. The app is free.
